---
title: A small family of apps
date: 2026-06-03
description: Curated, native, and built to feel like siblings rather than strangers.
---

I did not want a store with ten file managers in it. Singularity ships a small set of apps I actually use, and that is the point: enough to cover a day, nothing to wade through.

There is a file manager, [Files](/docs/apps/files/), with list, grid and column views. Two editors: [Edit](/docs/apps/edit/) for code, with Tree-sitter and the tabs down at the bottom so the screen is all code, and [Write](/docs/apps/write/) for markdown and reading PDFs. A terminal, [Leafs](/docs/apps/leafs/), that splits into panes and remembers your SSH boxes. [Music](/docs/apps/music/) that takes its color from the album art, [Photos](/docs/apps/photos/), a [video](/docs/apps/videos/) player, a [Calendar](/docs/apps/calendar/), a [Calculator](/docs/apps/calculator/), a [system monitor](/docs/apps/monitor/), and a [git client](/docs/apps/git/). A [keyring](/docs/apps/keyring/) keeps your secrets out of sight. And a [Store](/docs/apps/store/) for when you want more.

What ties them together is [libsingularity](/docs/libsingularity/): the same primitives, the same style, the same accent, the same bubble navigation. Moving between them feels like staying in one place, so learn one and you mostly know the rest.

They are not all the same age, and I would rather say so than pretend. Some are mature, some are young and rough. But they sit on the same foundation, which means they get better together. The full list, app by app, is in the [docs](/docs/apps/).
