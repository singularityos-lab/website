Ahead of releasing the Singularity Desktop source, and the chance to install it, sinty.dev has a new look. The style it had to wear was clear from the start: elegant, clean, and without noise, the exact philosophy behind the Singularity project.

I am using Astro and Starlight here, and I owe them a giant shout-out for how easy they make development.

Under the surface it carries the documentation: how to use the desktop, how to build on it, with real examples down to a working app. That is it, for now.
