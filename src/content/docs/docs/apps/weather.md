---
title: Weather
description: Forecasts, sunrise and sunset for the places you care about.
---

Weather shows the forecast for the places you care about.

- **Now**: the temperature, how it feels and why, wind and gusts, humidity,
  pressure, visibility and the UV index.
- **Next 24 hours** and **the next days**, with bars for the chance of rain.
- **Sunrise and sunset**, and how long the day is.

Add any city, town or village, or coordinates, and reorder the places. **Use My
Location** adds the weather where you are now. When the computer is offline, the
last forecast is shown with the time it was taken.

The data comes from Open-Meteo.com, under the CC BY 4.0 license. Only the
coordinates of your places are sent to Open-Meteo. Your current location comes
from the system location service and is used only when you ask for it.
