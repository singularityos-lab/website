title: Tasks
description: Plan what to do today and later, with reminders, a focus timer and shared lists.
---

Tasks keeps your to-dos in lists of your own.

## Adding tasks

Type a task the way you would say it: `Call Anna tomorrow at 5pm #work !high`
sets the due date and time, a tag and the priority in one line. A task can have
notes, subtasks, a reminder, and repeat every day, weekday, week, month or year.

**Today** shows what is due today or earlier, **Upcoming** what comes after.
Search by text, or by `#tag`.

## Reminders

Reminders arrive as notifications even when the Tasks window is closed: Tasks runs
in the background to deliver them.

## Focus timer

**Start Focus Timer** (Ctrl+T) on a task runs a focus session followed by a break.
Tasks keeps the time you focused on each task.

## Accounts and sharing

Lists live on this computer, or in a Google account added in Settings, Online
Accounts, and **Move to Account** moves a local list there. Offline changes sync
later. **Share List** invites other people to a list, so you work on it together.

## Moving tasks in and out

Import and export iCalendar files (only to-do items are imported, not events).
Deleted tasks stay in the trash until you empty it.

## For developers

Tasks offers the `dev.sinty.Tasks1` interface on the session bus, so other apps can
add tasks, mark them done and show them. Notes uses it for **Add to Tasks**.
