title: Wave
description: Record, edit, restore and mix audio.
---

Wave records, edits, restores and mixes audio.

- **Edit** a waveform: cut, amplify, fade, change time and pitch.
- **Restore** with adaptive noise reduction and other repair tools, guided by the
  spectrum.
- **Mix** multitrack sessions with effects racks, automation, buses and automatic
  ducking. Arm tracks to record.
- Loudness targets for broadcast, podcasts and audiobooks, and batch processing of
  many files.

Wave opens and saves WAV, Broadcast Wave, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, MP3, Ogg, Opus and AAC,
with chapters and cue points.
