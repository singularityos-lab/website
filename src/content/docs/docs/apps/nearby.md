---
title: Nearby
description: Send files, links and messages between the computer, your phone and nearby devices.
---

Nearby connects the computer with your phone and with other computers on the
same network. It speaks the KDE Connect protocol, so it works with KDE Connect on
Android and iPhone and with GSConnect on other computers.

## Pairing

Open KDE Connect on the phone, keep it on the same network, and choose **Pair a
Device**. Both devices show a code: pair only if they match. A device that the
network hides from discovery can be added by its address.

## What you can do

- **Send Files** and **Send Link** to a device, or drop files on the window. A
  link opens on the phone.
- **Received Files** lists what devices sent to this computer. Files from a device
  you trust can be accepted without asking.
- **Messages** reads and sends the phone's text messages.
- **Notifications** from the phone show up in the app and in the notification
  center, and you can reply from them.
- **Shared Clipboard**: copy on one device, paste on the other.
- **Now Playing** controls music playing on the phone, **Ring** makes it ring, and
  its battery level is shown.

Nearby also shares files from Files and other apps through the share menu.
