---
title: Slides
description: Create and present slideshows.
---

Slides creates and presents slideshows.

- Start from a theme or a template, such as a pitch, an agenda or a lesson.
- Text, shapes, pictures, tables, charts, transitions and animations, with
  action buttons.
- **Present** with the slides advancing on click or by themselves, and rehearse
  timings.
- An accessibility check helps every audience follow along.

Presentations open and save as PowerPoint (`.pptx`, `.ppsx`, `.potx`) and
OpenDocument (`.odp`). A slide exports as SVG, and all slides as images.
