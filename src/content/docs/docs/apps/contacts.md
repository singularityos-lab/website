---
title: Contacts
description: Your address book, on this computer and in your online accounts.
---

Contacts keeps the people you know: names, phone numbers, email and postal
addresses, birthdays, company, notes and a photo.

- Contacts live on this computer or in an online account, such as Google or
  Nextcloud, added in Settings, Online Accounts. Offline changes sync later.
- Mark people you contact often as **favorites**.
- From a contact you can call, send an email, see your recent mail with them, and
  show their address on a map.
- **Import** and **export** vCard files, or save a single contact as a vCard to
  share it.

[QR Codes](/docs/apps/qr-codes/) fills a contact card code from Contacts.
