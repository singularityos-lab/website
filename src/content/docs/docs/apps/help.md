---
title: Help
description: Guides for the desktop, your apps and your system, and every command manual.
---

Help collects the guides that the desktop, your apps and your system install,
and the manual of every command-line program.

- **Guides** are grouped by app and topic. Links between pages work like in a web
  browser, with **Back** and **Forward**.
- **Command Manuals** list every manual page installed on the system.
- **Search** looks for a topic or a command across both.

Apps open Help at their own guide from their Help menu.
