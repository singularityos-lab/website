---
title: Radio
description: Listen to internet radio stations.
---

Radio plays internet radio stations.

- Browse stations by country, genre or language, or search by name.
- Keep the stations you like in your library, and see what played recently.
- Listen to any stream by its address.
- Copy the title of the song that is playing.
- A sleep timer stops playback after a time or at the end of the song.
- **Radio alarms** wake you up with a station at a time and on the days you
  choose. They need [Clock](/docs/apps/clock/).
