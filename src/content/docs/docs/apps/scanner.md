---
title: Document Scanner
description: Scan documents and photos to PDF or images, with searchable text.
---

Document Scanner scans paper into PDF documents and images. It finds scanners
connected to the computer and on the network.

- Scan from the **glass** one page at a time, or feed a stack through the
  **document feeder**, on one side or both.
- Choose color, grayscale or black and white, the quality in dpi and the paper
  size.
- Rotate pages, change their order and delete the ones you do not want before
  saving.

Save as a **PDF**, or a page as PNG or JPEG. PDFs get searchable text: Scanner
recognizes the text on each page, and you can also copy it or send it to Write.
