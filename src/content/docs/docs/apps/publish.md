---
title: Publish
description: Lay out flyers, brochures, newsletters and cards for print.
---

Publish lays out documents for print: flyers, brochures, posters, newsletters,
programs, invoices, cards and labels.

- Start from a template, then place text frames, pictures and tables on pages
  with master pages, columns and a baseline grid.
- Text flows between frames, with paragraph and character styles.
- **Merge fields** fill labels and cards from a CSV file or a sheet of an XLSX or
  ODS workbook.
- Print-ready output with bleed, slug and marks, and an accessibility checker for
  accessible PDF.

Publish opens its own files, Scribus documents, InDesign IDML and Publisher files,
and exports as PDF, Scribus, IDML, ODG and Word.
