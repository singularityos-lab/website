Atelier is for fashion design: sketch garments, make patterns, grade sizes and
prepare tech packs.

- **Sketch** flats and illustrations, with colorways.
- **Patterns** drawn from measurements and formulas such as `bust_circ / 4`, with
  seam allowances, linings and pieces nested for cutting.
- **Grading** to every size with grade rules, including the ASTM ones.
- **Simulation** of the pattern pieces on an avatar.
- **Tech packs** with the bill of materials, fabrics, trims and colors.

Patterns exchange as DXF (AAMA and ASTM), Seamly2D and Valentina files; sketches
and flats export as SVG, tables as XLSX or CSV, and the tech pack as a Write
document.
