---
title: Reader
description: Read, annotate, fill and sign PDF documents.
---

Reader opens PDF documents and works on them.

- **Annotate** with highlights, notes, shapes and arrows, and exchange comments
  with others.
- **Fill forms** and **sign**, or ask others to sign.
- **Organize pages**: insert, delete, rotate and reorder them, add blank pages,
  bookmarks and attachments.
- **Redact** text and areas so it is removed from the file, not only covered.
- **Protect** a document with a password and choose what others may do: print,
  copy, edit, fill forms or comment.
- Convert to PDF/A, add Bates numbering, compare documents, and run batch actions
  on many files.
- Export pages to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, or data to CSV.
