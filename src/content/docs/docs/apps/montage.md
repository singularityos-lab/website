---
title: Montage
description: Edit video on a multitrack timeline.
---

Montage edits video on a multitrack timeline.

- Bins for your media, cuts, transitions, titles and captions.
- Speed changes and ramps, color correction, blur and other effects, motion
  analysis.
- Audio tracks and buses, with automatic ducking under voices and loudness targets
  for broadcast (EBU R128, ATSC A/85).
- An export queue with presets for the web, social networks, podcasts and masters
  such as ProRes and lossless archives.

Timelines exchange with other editors as OTIO, EDL, FCPXML, Premiere, AAF and
Kdenlive projects.
