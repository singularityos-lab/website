---
title: Formula
description: Write equations for documents, slides and the web.
---

Formula writes mathematical equations.

- Build an equation from a palette of fractions, roots, integrals, matrices,
  accents, arrows and more, or type LaTeX commands.
- A gallery of common equations, from the area of a circle to Bayes' theorem, and
  chemistry formulas.
- Aligned equations, long equations broken to fit the page.

Copy an equation as an image, as MathML, as LaTeX or as a Word equation, and
export it as SVG, MathML, LaTeX or a Word document. Formula also opens MathML,
OpenDocument formulas and LaTeX.
