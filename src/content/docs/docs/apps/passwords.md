---
title: Passwords
description: Keep your logins in the Singularity keyring, encrypted and always at hand.
---

Passwords shows and edits the logins kept in the
[keyring](/docs/apps/keyring/), encrypted with your login password.

- Items are grouped into websites, apps and networks, with a search.
- **New Password** (Ctrl+N) saves a login with a strong generated password. The
  generator makes random characters or words, with the length and the kinds of
  characters you choose, and rates the strength of every password.
- A copied password is cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
- **Lock** (Ctrl+L) locks the keyring until you unlock it again.

**Import from CSV** reads a file exported by a browser or another password manager.
**Export to CSV** writes your passwords as plain text: anyone who opens the file can
read them.

Several keyrings can be kept side by side, each locked separately.
