---
title: Phone
description: Make and receive voice calls with the modem of the device.
---

Phone makes and receives voice calls on devices with a built-in modem, such as
the [Pixel 7 Pro](/docs/os/devices/pixel-7-pro/). On a computer without a modem
that can make calls, it says so and does nothing else.

- **Keypad**: dial a number and choose **Call**.
- **Recents** lists the calls you made and received, with **Missed** calls on
  their own. Pick one to call back, **Send Message** from
  [Messages](/docs/apps/messages/), **Copy Number** or **Remove from Recents**.
- **Incoming Call**: choose **Accept** or **Decline**. A call you do not answer
  becomes a **Missed Call** notification.
- **During a call**: **Mute**, the keypad for menus that ask for a number, and
  **End Call**.

Calls stay on this device until they end. The modem is reached through the
ModemManager interface, which Sinty OS serves with its own modem service; see
[Mobile](/docs/os/mobile/) for the SIM, the eSIM and mobile data.
