---
title: Disk Usage
description: See which folders and files take up space.
---

Disk Usage shows what takes up space on your drives and in your folders.

Scan your **home folder**, the **system drive**, or any folder or drive you pick.
The result is a ring chart: point at a ring for details, click a folder to open it
and click the center to go up. Show an item in Files, copy its path, or move it to
the trash from the same place.
