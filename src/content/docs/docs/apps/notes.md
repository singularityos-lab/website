Notes keeps your notes in **notebooks**, split into **sections** and **pages**,
with subpages, tags and pinned pages.

## Writing

A page holds formatted text, lists, to-dos, tables, links, pictures, attachments
and equations. **Templates** start a page with a layout: meeting notes, lecture
notes in the Cornell layout, a journal, a brainstorm, a decision with pros and
cons, a project overview and more. A **free-form page** lets you place note
containers anywhere.

- **Draw** with a pen, a highlighter and shapes, select with a lasso and erase.
  Handwriting and shapes can be recognized.
- **Copy Text from Picture** reads the text in a picture or a scanned page.
- **Insert Printout** places the pages of a PDF in a page.
- **Record Audio** links a recording to your notes, and its transcript can be
  added to the page.
- The **Math Assistant** solves equations step by step and draws their graph.

## Finding and organizing

Every tagged paragraph and every to-do of your notebooks is listed in one place.
**Add to Tasks** sends a to-do to [Tasks](/docs/apps/tasks/). Links can point to a
page, a section or a single paragraph.

## Keeping notes safe

Sections can be protected with a password. **Page Versions** brings back an
earlier version of a page, and deleted pages stay in the recycle bin for 60 days.

## Sync and sharing

Notes stay on this computer, or sync with Nextcloud Notes or a folder on WebDAV.
Notebooks can be shared through an online account with files, and a **Live
Session** lets several people edit the same page at once. **Phone and Browser
Access** opens your notes from a phone or a browser on the local network.

## Export

Export a page or a section as PDF, OpenDocument Text, Word, Markdown or pictures.
