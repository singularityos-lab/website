---
title: Camera
description: Take photos and record videos with the computer's camera.
---

Camera takes photos and records videos.

- Press **Space** to take a photo or to start and stop a recording. Switch between
  **Photo** and **Video** below the preview.
- A **timer** of 3 or 10 seconds delays the shot.
- **Switch Camera** moves between cameras when there is more than one, and **Look
  for Cameras Again** finds one connected after the app started.
- The preview can be mirrored, like a mirror, or shown as the camera sees it.

Photos are saved in Pictures/Camera and videos in Videos/Camera. **Open Last Shot** shows the
latest one, and it can be shared from the same place.

When the camera sees a QR code, Camera recognizes it and offers what it holds: a
web address, a Wi-Fi network, a contact card, a calendar event or a two-factor
code. **Open in QR Codes** hands it over to [QR Codes](/docs/apps/qr-codes/).
