---
title: Maps
description: Find places and plan routes, with OpenStreetMap data.
---

Maps finds places and plans routes.

- Search for a town, an address, a shop or coordinates, or browse places nearby
  such as cafés, pharmacies, stations and hospitals.
- **Directions** plans a route by car, bike or on foot, with turn-by-turn steps.
- Mark places with the star to keep them in **Favorite Places**, and drop a pin
  to copy its coordinates or a link.
- **My Location** centers the map where you are, when location is allowed.
- Switch between the street map, terrain and transport views. **Photos Taken
  Here** shows your photos taken at a place.

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap, and routes from OSRM.
