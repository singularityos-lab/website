---
title: Printers
description: Follow print jobs and printer alerts.
---

Printers shows what you are printing and tells you when a printer needs
attention. Printers are added and set up in Settings; see
[Printing](/docs/os/printing/).

- **Waiting to Print** lists the documents in the queue, with the page being
  printed. Hold, resume or cancel a document, or pause the whole printer.
- **Recently Printed** lists finished and cancelled documents.
- **Supplies** shows ink or toner levels once the printer has printed something.

Printers runs in the background and sends a notification when a printer is
running low or needs attention. Fix it on the printer and printing continues by itself.
