Tour is a short slideshow of how the desktop works: the top bar, the launcher, the
dock, arranging windows, workspaces, getting more apps from the Store, making the
desktop yours in Settings, and where to find help. Open it at any time from the
app list.

For anything longer, the last slide opens [Help](/docs/apps/help/).
