---
title: Podcasts
description: Listen to your favorite shows.
---

Podcasts follows shows and plays their episodes.

- **Discover** shows, or add one by its feed address.
- Download episodes to listen offline, and queue them in **Up Next**.
- Skip forward and back, change the playback speed, and jump between chapters.
- A sleep timer stops playback after a time, at the end of the episode or of the
  chapter.
- Episodes you started and did not finish are kept for later.

Subscriptions import and export as OPML.
