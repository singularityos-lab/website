---
title: Voice
description: Record voice notes and meetings, with transcripts.
---

Voice records voice notes and meetings.

- Choose the microphone and record, pause and resume.
- Recordings get a transcript you can search and copy, where the system supports
  transcription.
- Trim a recording to the part you want, or save the selection as a new recording.
- Play at a different speed and skip 10 seconds forward or back.
- Export as Opus, FLAC, MP3, WAV or AAC, share a recording, or send it to
  [Notes](/docs/apps/notes/).
