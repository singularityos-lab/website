Calculator covers both quick sums and the longer expressions you reach for less
often.

## Two modes

A toggle switches between Basic and Advanced. Basic is the digits, the four
operations, percent, sign change, and a decimal point. Advanced adds the
scientific functions: sine, cosine, and tangent, base-ten logarithm, square root,
powers, factorial, parentheses for grouping, the constant pi, and a switch
between degrees and radians.

A line above the result keeps the running expression in view, and division by
zero and other invalid input show as an error rather than a wrong answer.

## Typing

You can drive it entirely from the keyboard: the digits and operators, `Enter` or
`=` to evaluate, `Backspace` to delete, and `Escape` or `Delete` to clear.

Calculator is also available as a [widget](/docs/widgets/), so you can keep one on
hand without opening the full app.
