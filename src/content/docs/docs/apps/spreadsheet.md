---

Spreadsheet works with numbers, formulas and charts.

- Formulas with the functions you expect, from sums and conditions to statistics,
  finance, dates and engineering.
- Charts, conditional formatting with color scales and data bars, tables with
  totals, filters and data validation.
- Analysis tools such as regression, and tracked changes with accept and reject.
- Protected sheets and ranges that only some people can edit.

Files open and save as Excel (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.xlsb`, `.xls`, `.xltx`),
OpenDocument (`.ods`) and CSV. Excel VBA modules are kept.
