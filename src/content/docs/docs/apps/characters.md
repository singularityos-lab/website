---
title: Characters
description: Find and copy special characters and emoji.
---

Characters finds a character and copies it, so you can paste it anywhere.

- **Emoji** are grouped as on a phone: smileys, people, animals, food, travel,
  activities, objects, symbols and flags, with skin tones.
- **Symbols** cover punctuation, arrows, math, currency, shapes, Latin, Greek and
  Cyrillic letters.
- **Search** by name, or by a code such as `U+263A`.

Double click a character or press **Enter** to copy it. **Copy Code** copies its
code point instead. The characters you copy are kept under **Recently Used**.
