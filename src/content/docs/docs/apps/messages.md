---
title: Messages
description: All your conversations in one place, from your online accounts and your phone.
---

Messages brings the conversations of your accounts together in one window.

- Chat accounts added in Settings, Online Accounts bring their conversations here
  by themselves.
- **Phone (SMS)** reads and sends the text messages of a phone paired with
  [Nearby](/docs/apps/nearby/).

Start a **New Conversation** (Ctrl+N) by name, address or phone number, or create
a group with people you already write to. Reply to a message, react to it, edit
or delete it, insert emoji and send files. Conversations can be muted, marked as
read and opened in a window of their own.
