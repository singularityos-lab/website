---
title: Authenticator
description: Two-factor codes for your accounts.
---

Authenticator shows the one-time codes that your accounts ask for when you sign
in with two-factor authentication.

## Adding accounts

- **Read a QR code** from a screenshot or a picture.
- Paste an `otpauth://` setup link, or type the secret key.
- **Import** accounts from an Aegis backup, a Google Authenticator export or a
  list of links.

Time-based codes (TOTP) and counter-based codes (HOTP) with 6, 7 or 8 digits are
supported.

## Using codes

Search for an account and press Enter to copy its code. Up to three accounts you
use often can be added to the dock menu, to copy a code without opening the
window.

## Keeping them safe

Accounts are stored in the keyring. A lock password keeps them hidden until you
unlock Authenticator. **Back Up with a Password** writes an encrypted backup, and
a single account can be shown as a QR code to move it to another device.
