---
title: Mail
description: Read and write email from IMAP, Exchange and JMAP accounts.
---

Mail, also called Lettere, reads and writes email.

- **Accounts**: IMAP, Exchange and JMAP, added in the app or from Settings, Online
  Accounts. **All Inboxes** shows every account together.
- **Writing**: formatted messages, attachments, signatures per account, Cc and
  Bcc, and recipients from a CSV file or a group in Contacts.
- **Organizing**: categories, favorites, archive, rules with conditions and
  actions, blocked senders and unsubscribe.
- **Calendar and tasks**: accept invitations and time proposals, add an event to
  Calendar or a message to [Tasks](/docs/apps/tasks/), add a sender to
  [Contacts](/docs/apps/contacts/).
- **Automatic replies** when you are away.
- **Security**: signed and encrypted mail with OpenPGP keys and S/MIME
  certificates.

Messages import and export as `.eml` and `.mbox`, and Outlook data files (`.pst`)
and messages (`.msg`) open too.
