---
title: Photos
description: The Singularity photo viewer.
---

Photos shows the images in your library and gives you a clean way to look through
them.

## Your library

By default Photos watches your Pictures folder, including subfolders, and lays
the images out as a grid of thumbnails whose size you can change. The sidebar
sorts them into Library, Favorites, Recently added, and Trash, and a search box
filters by filename. New images that land in the folder show up on their own.

## Viewing

Click a photo to open the viewer. Its controls are [bubbles](/docs/bubbles/) that
float over the image and stay out of the way of the photo itself. Move between
photos with the left and right arrow keys, and close the viewer with `Escape`.
You can rotate left or right, zoom in, and mark a photo as a favorite with the
star. The filename, date, and size sit along the bottom.

## Getting photos in and out

Import copies images into your library from anywhere, and you can drag images
onto the window to bring them in. A right-click gives you open, copy to
clipboard, favorite, and move to Trash.

Photos opens JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, and HEIC.

:::note
Photos is for viewing and organizing. The only edit it makes is rotating the
view, and that rotation is not written back to the file.
:::
