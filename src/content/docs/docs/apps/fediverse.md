---
title: Fediverse
description: Follow people on Mastodon and the social web.
---

Fediverse is a client for Mastodon and the other servers of the social web.

- Add one or more accounts, and read your home timeline, notifications,
  conversations and bookmarks.
- Write posts with images and videos, descriptions for people who cannot see them,
  content warnings and polls. Save drafts and schedule posts.
- Boost, bookmark and reply. Follow people, or ask to follow locked accounts.
- Filter out words and phrases, block accounts and whole servers.
