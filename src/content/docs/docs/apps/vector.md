---
title: Vector
description: Create illustrations, icons and scalable graphics.
---

Vector creates illustrations, icons and other scalable graphics.

- Pen and shape tools, compound paths, clipping and opacity masks.
- Gradients, freeform gradients and gradient meshes.
- Several **artboards** in one document, for icon sets or screens.
- **Symbols**, including dynamic symbols whose colors change per instance.
- **Image Trace** turns a picture into paths.

Export as SVG, compressed SVG, PDF, EPS and images, or every artboard at once with
**Export for Screens**.
