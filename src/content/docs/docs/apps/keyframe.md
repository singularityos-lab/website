title: Keyframe
description: Animate titles, graphics and effects, and composite video.
---

Keyframe animates titles, graphics and effects, and composites video.

- Compositions with layers, masks, mattes and adjustment layers.
- Keyframes and easing on any property, text animators, shape layers.
- Effects, lights, cameras and color management with ACES.
- A render queue, with video formats such as ProRes 4444 with alpha, animated GIF
  and audio.

Animations import and export as Lottie, for apps and the web.
