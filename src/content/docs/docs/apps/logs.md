---
title: Logs
description: Read the messages the system and apps write.
---

Logs shows the messages that the system, its services and your apps write, so
you can see what went wrong.

- **Problems** shows the warnings and errors of the current session.
- **Your Apps**, **Services** and **System** group messages by where they come
  from. Pick a service to read only its messages.
- The **kernel log** is protected; reading it needs authorization.
- Earlier sessions are kept, back to the start of the computer.

Filter by level, from errors only to everything including debug messages, search
the text, and turn on **Follow New Messages** to watch them as they arrive. A
message shows its process, command line and source. **Export** saves the
messages you are looking at to a file.
