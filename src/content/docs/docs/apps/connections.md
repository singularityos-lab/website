---
title: Connections
description: See and control other computers over VNC and RDP.
---

Connections shows the screen of another computer and lets you control it, over
VNC or RDP.

- **New Connection** takes the address of a computer, and an `.rdp` or `.vnc`
  file opens as a connection.
- Passwords can be remembered in the keyring.
- Fit the remote screen to the window, show it at its original size, or resize the
  remote desktop to match the window. **View Only** watches without controlling.
- **Send Keys** sends Ctrl+Alt+Del and other combinations the local desktop would
  keep, and **Take a Screenshot** saves the remote screen.

The first connection to a computer shows its identity so you can trust it.
