title: Apps
description: The first-party apps that ship with Singularity.
---

Singularity ships a small set of first-party apps. The aim is to cover the
everyday rather than to fill a catalog. They all build on
[libsingularity](/docs/libsingularity/), so they share the same look, the same
primitives, and your accent color.

## The lineup

- [Files](/docs/apps/files/), the file manager.
- [Edit](/docs/apps/edit/), the code and text editor.
- [Write](/docs/apps/write/), the markdown editor and PDF reader.
- [Store](/docs/apps/store/), where you find more apps.
- [Monitor](/docs/apps/monitor/), the system monitor.
- [Git](/docs/apps/git/), the git client.
- [Music](/docs/apps/music/), the music player.
- [Photos](/docs/apps/photos/), the photo viewer.
- [Videos](/docs/apps/videos/), the video player.
- [Calendar](/docs/apps/calendar/), your calendar.
- [Calculator](/docs/apps/calculator/), the calculator.
- [Leafs](/docs/apps/leafs/), the terminal.
- [Keyring](/docs/apps/keyring/), the secret store that works behind the scenes.
- [Browser](/docs/apps/browser/), the web browser.
- [Backups](/docs/apps/backups/), automatic backups of your files.
- [Camera](/docs/apps/camera/), photos and videos.
- [Characters](/docs/apps/characters/), special characters and emoji.
- [Clock](/docs/apps/clock/), alarms, world clocks, stopwatch, timer and Pomodoro.
- [Color Picker](/docs/apps/color-picker/), colors, palettes and contrast.
- [Disks](/docs/apps/disks/), drives, partitions and disk images.
- [Disk Usage](/docs/apps/disk-usage/), what takes up space.
- [Document Scanner](/docs/apps/scanner/), paper to searchable PDF.
- [Fonts](/docs/apps/fonts/), browse and install fonts.
- [Help](/docs/apps/help/), guides and command manuals.
- [Logs](/docs/apps/logs/), the messages the system writes.
- [Markup](/docs/apps/markup/), annotate screenshots.
- [Nearby](/docs/apps/nearby/), your phone and nearby devices.
- [Printers](/docs/apps/printers/), print jobs and printer alerts.
- [QR Codes](/docs/apps/qr-codes/), scan and create codes.
- [Tour](/docs/apps/tour/), a first look at the desktop.
- [Weather](/docs/apps/weather/), forecasts for your places.

- [Connections](/docs/apps/connections/), remote desktops over VNC and RDP.

- [Machines](/docs/apps/machines/), virtual machines.
- [Mail](/docs/apps/mail/), email.

The office suite:

- [Slides](/docs/apps/slides/), presentations.
- [Database](/docs/apps/database/), tables, queries, forms and reports.

The creative suite:

- [Montage](/docs/apps/montage/), video editing.
- [Keyframe](/docs/apps/keyframe/), motion graphics.
- [Wave](/docs/apps/wave/), audio editing.
- [Atelier](/docs/apps/atelier/), fashion design and patterns.

They are not all the same age. Some are mature, some are young. They share a
foundation, which means they improve together.
