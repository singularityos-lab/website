---
title: Public alerts and emergencies
description: Official warnings and earthquakes for where you are, the emergency screen, and the medical details shown on it.
---

Sinty OS can follow the public alerts of the authorities of your country and two
earthquake catalogues, and keeps an emergency screen one tap away. Set it up in
**Settings, Safety**. The service behind it is Sinty Aegis; if it is not running,
the page says so.

## Public alerts

Turn on **Follow Public Alerts**. Alerts are matched to the place this computer
is in:

- a **danger** turns the top bar red, sounds an alarm and shows an alert card for
  five minutes;
- everything else is a quiet line in the top bar.

**Show Alerts From** picks the lowest level shown: Minor, Moderate, Severe or
Extreme. **Earthquakes From Magnitude** sets the smallest earthquake that counts;
only earthquakes that can be felt where you are are shown.

**Sources** lists the feeds found for your country. Where no authority publishes
alerts online, earthquakes still work. A severe weather alert opens Weather.

## Your place

Leave **Your Place** empty to use the country and city of your time zone, or set:

- **Country**, as two letters, like `it` or `us`;
- **Regions**, with the names your authority uses, separated by commas;
- **Position**, as latitude and longitude, like `41.90, 12.50`.

## The emergency screen

Every alert card opens the emergency screen. It shows the emergency numbers for
your country and, if you set them:

- an **Emergency Contact** and their phone number;
- your **Medical Details**: name, blood type, allergies, conditions and
  medication.

Medical details are kept on this computer only. Anyone can read them from the
emergency screen once **Show on the Emergency Screen** is on.

## Trying it

**Send a Test Danger** shows the red top bar and the alert card for five minutes.
**Send a Quiet Test** shows only a line in the top bar.
