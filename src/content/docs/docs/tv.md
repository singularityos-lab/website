---

When a screen behaves as a TV, Singularity swaps the desktop for an interface
made to be read from the sofa and driven with a remote, a keyboard or a game
pad. To make a screen a TV, see [Screens and form factors](/docs/screens/).

## The home

The TV home opens on a greeting and a **Featured** row of films and series.
Each title says which of your streaming apps has it, under **Available on**, and
**Watch** opens it there. Titles not offered by any of your apps in your region
are left out.

Below:

- **Continue watching** brings back what you were playing;
- **Apps** holds the apps made for the TV, shown as coloured tiles;
- **All Apps** opens every other app in a folder.

When the computer is offline the home says so and keeps your apps available.

## Moving around

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows | move the focus |
| OK or Enter | open what has focus |
| OK held | the menu of the item, like a right click |
| Back, Escape or Backspace | go back one step; on the top level, back to the home |
| Menu | the menu of the app |

Focus returns to the item you left when you come back. Text fields open a
keyboard you drive with the remote, and the on-screen keyboard follows the
remote too. A phone can act as the remote: see
[A phone next to a bigger screen](/docs/screens/#a-phone-next-to-a-bigger-screen).

## Apps on a TV

Apps made for the TV take a layout for it: full screen, a dark theme, a header
with the sections of the app as large tabs, a search field and the actions of
dialogs, and no window controls. Back follows the app's own back button.

Apps made for the TV say so, as Weather and Tour do, and so do web apps
installed for the TV, such as Netflix or YouTube. See [Web apps](/docs/web-apps/).

An app that lists the screens it is made for and leaves the TV out still opens,
in **compatibility mode**: it keeps its normal layout, and the remote moves a
pointer instead of a focus. The arrows move the pointer, faster the longer they
are held, and OK clicks.

The **Apps** row on the home shows the apps made for the TV, and players, music,
video, games, photos and similar apps that say nothing either way.

## Settings

**Settings** on the TV home opens a grid of the settings that matter on a TV.
**All Settings** opens the full Settings app.

## Power

A TV is treated as a power saving device. The TV interface draws at 30 frames per
second while no app is on screen, and idle background apps are frozen as they
are on battery. See [Pausing apps](/docs/os/pausing-apps/).
