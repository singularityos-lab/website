---
title: Notifications
description: How notifications appear, how they are enriched and grouped, and how to quiet them.
---

Notifications in Singularity arrive as small pop-ups and are kept in the
notification center so you can come back to them.

## Pop-ups

When an app sends a notification, a pop-up slides in from the top-right corner. It
shows an icon, a summary, and a body, along with any actions the app offers.
Pop-ups dismiss themselves after a few seconds, or you can close one yourself. If
an app updates a notification it already sent, the pop-up updates in place rather
than stacking a second one.

## Enriched icons

A notification is more useful when it looks like what it is about. Singularity
picks the most specific image a notification carries rather than always falling
back to the app's logo: if the notification ships its own image, a path or raw
image data, that is what you see. So a chat message can show the contact's avatar
and a player can show the album art, and only when there is no such image does it
fall back to the sending app's icon.

## The notification center

Everything that arrives is kept in the notification center, reached from the
panel, where the button carries a badge with how many you have not seen yet.

The center groups notifications by app. A single notification shows as one row;
when an app has sent several, they collapse into a group with a header that shows
the app's icon, its name, a preview of the latest, and a count. Expand the group
to read them all, clear a whole group from its header, or clear everything at once.
When there is nothing left, the center simply says so.

## Do Not Disturb

Do Not Disturb stops the pop-ups from appearing without losing anything: the
notifications still arrive and are kept in the center, they just do not interrupt
you. The panel reflects the mode so you know it is on.
