---
title: Profiles and focus
description: Profiles change a chosen set of settings, such as who can disturb you, and turn on by hand or by themselves at a time, on a network, with a screen or a game.
---

A profile changes only what you pick. Everything left at **Don't Change** stays
as you left it, and every change is put back when the profile ends. Manage them
in **Settings, Profiles**.

## Making a profile

Choose **Add Profile…**. A new profile starts with everything set to
**Don't Change**; set only what it should change. Two shortcuts help:

- **Save As It Is Now** gives every setting the value it has right now;
- **Change Nothing** sets everything back to Don't Change.

A profile can also **Open Apps** and **Close Apps** when it turns on, and have a
**Keyboard Shortcut**.

## Who can disturb

**Silence Notifications** keeps notifications quiet in the notification centre
while the profile is on. Exceptions come through:

- **Allowed People**: notifications that name these contacts;
- **Allowed Apps**: apps that can always notify you;
- **Time-Sensitive Notifications** from apps that mark them so;
- **Repeated Calls**: a second call from the same person within three minutes.

**Show Badges in the Dock** keeps unread counts on app icons, and
**Mute Notification Sounds** keeps even the notifications that come through
quiet.

## Turning on

- **Turn On Now** keeps the profile on until you turn it off or pick another.
- **Turn On Automatically** adds rules: a time, a Wi-Fi network, a screen, a
  device, a game, a Sinty Key, or the battery.

When more than one profile could turn on, the one with the most specific rule
wins. **Tell Me When It Changes** sends a notification saying which profile
turned on and why.
