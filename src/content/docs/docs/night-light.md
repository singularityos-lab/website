---
title: Night Light
description: Warm the screen in the evening, on a schedule or always, and switch to the dark appearance with it.
---

Night Light warms the colours of the screen to make it easier on the eyes in the
evening. Set it in **Settings, Displays, Night Light**.

**Schedule** chooses when it is on:

- **Always**;
- **Sunset to Sunrise**, worked out from your location, or from your time zone
  when the location is unknown;
- **Custom Times**, from one hour to another.

**Temperature** sets how warm the screen gets, in Kelvin: lower values are
warmer.

**Dark Theme** also switches to the dark appearance during the schedule.
