---
title: Appearance
description: Wallpaper, dark mode, accent color, and themes.
---

Appearance in Singularity is a handful of decisions that carry across the whole
desktop. You will find them in the desktop settings.

## Wallpaper

Choose a **Theme pack** and then a **Wallpaper pack**, or select your own
image. The wallpaper you set on the desktop also shows up, blurred, on the
login screen.

Wallpaper sources are providers supplied by plugins. Singularity ships stock,
Bing, and OCS wallpaper plugins. The OCS and Bing sources are opt-in and
disabled by default. After enabling one in Plugins, use **Explore Wallpaper
Sources**, described in the interface as **Browse sources enabled from
Plugins**.

## Dark mode

Dark mode is a single toggle. Flip it and the whole desktop changes together,
apps included, and your accent comes with it.

## Accent color

The accent is the color the desktop uses for buttons, switches, sliders, selected
rows, and the borders around your windows. There are three ways to set it:

- **Named colors** give you a set of dependable choices: blue, teal, green,
  yellow, orange, red, pink, purple, and slate.
- **From the wallpaper** reads the dominant tone of your current wallpaper and
  uses it as the accent.
- **Custom** opens a picker with a saturation and value field, a hue bar, a hex
  entry, and an eyedropper for sampling a color from the screen.

A live preview shows the accent in context before you commit to it, and the
choice is applied everywhere at once.

## Themes

Beyond the accent, you can change the look more deeply:

- **Singularity theme** picks the style applied to Singularity apps, with any
  user themes you have installed listed alongside the built-in default.
- A fine-tuning section lets you set the GTK 3, GTK 4, and Qt themes separately,
  so apps from across the toolkits can be brought into line. The Qt option
  appears when a Kvantum-based setup is available.

## Window buttons

You can also choose which side the window buttons sit on, and whether the close,
minimize, and maximize buttons appear, with a live titlebar preview as you
change it.
